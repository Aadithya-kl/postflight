# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the PostFlight community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and open-source ecosystem

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via private repository security/maintainer contact channels. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
