We analyzed 9 social platforms and found why great posts silently fail:

LinkedIn folds mobile posts at line 3 and counts blank lines as full lines. Instagram slashed hashtags from 30 to 5 and slices 34px off 4:5 images in its 3:4 profile grid.

PostFlight catches reach killers, UI overlay collisions, WCAG issues, and FTC fold violations locally before you ever publish.
